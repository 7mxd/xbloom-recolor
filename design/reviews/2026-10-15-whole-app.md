# Design review: xBloom Recolor (whole app), 2026-10-15

**10 findings: 1 critical, 1 major, 8 minor. 6 fixed, 4 kept on purpose.**

The three that mattered most:

1. **F-002 (major, fixed):** the first screen asked for a share link without saying where it comes from. It now says: share the recipe from the xBloom app and paste the link, which looks like `https://share-h5.xbloom.com/?id=…`.
2. **F-001 (critical, kept on purpose):** the card preview's cup type ("Tea") is faint (1.73:1) because the preview copies the xBloom card. The cup type is now written in the summary line and read out by screen readers; the faint text itself stays.
3. **F-005 (minor, fixed):** on phones the pours table broke values in half ("100 / ml"). Values now stay on one line, with the pour pattern on a quieter line below.

Screenshots: before in `design/shots/before/`, after in `design/shots/` and `design/shots/after/`, side by side in `design/shots/compare/before/`. The editor (shown after a recipe opens) was captured with a sample, made-up recipe.

## Critical

- **F-001 · contrast · `public/index.html:84`, the editor (`.card .cup`).** The card preview's cup type is 1.73:1 on the card in light and dark; the cup type wasn't written anywhere else. **Fix:** keep the preview identical to the xBloom card, and give its content in words. **Done:** the summary line starts with the cup type, and the preview is announced as "Card preview: Tea, 3 pours". **Status:** open: the faint text stays by design, so the audit still reports it. Shot: `design/shots/before/editor.mobile.light.png`.

## Major

- **F-002 · missing-state · `public/index.html:175`, `/`.** The first screen never said where a share link comes from or what it looks like. **Fix:** a line under the field. **Fixed.** Shot: `design/shots/before/index.mobile.png`.

## Minor

- **F-003 · target-size · `#theme-toggle`, `/`.** The theme toggle was 40×40 px. **Fixed:** 44×44 px.
- **F-004 · target-size · "Custom colour", the editor.** It opened from a 20 px tall line. **Fixed:** the whole row is 44 px tall.
- **F-005 · typography · the pours table, the editor.** Values broke in half on phones. **Fixed:** values stay on one line, the pattern sits below in the secondary colour, and narrow screens get tighter cell padding.
- **F-006 · missing-state · "Copy link".** No failure state, and "Copied" stayed forever. **Fixed:** says when copying fails and suggests selecting the link; returns to "Copy link" after two seconds.
- **F-007 · platform-convention · the sliders.** Firefox showed no focus ring on them. **Fixed:** the same ring as in Chrome and Safari.
- **F-008 · target-size · the 16 swatches (31×31 px on a phone).** **Kept:** all 16 fit in two rows, and with 10 px gaps they pass the 24 px minimum.
- **F-009 · target-size · the sliders (26 px tall).** **Kept:** full width, easy to drag, above the 24 px minimum.
- **F-010 · consistency · the page's styles.** Seven text sizes for six type roles (14 px has none) and spacing off the 4 px scale. **Kept:** recorded in `DESIGN.md`; new work uses its roles and scale.

## Checks

- `motif validate`: the design guide is valid (104 theme values, 26 readability checks in light and dark).
- `motif shot --audit` on `/`: no problems after the fixes (before: the 40 px theme toggle).
- The editor's audit after the fixes: the card's faint cup type (kept), the swatches and sliders (kept); "Custom colour" fixed.
- `motif check` and `motif extract` don't support pages without a framework yet, so the design guide was written by hand from the page's CSS.
- The app's own tests: 4 of 4 pass.
